Zero tolerance for abuse

You may only send tapes to people who added you back. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users: no harassment, hate, threats, sexual content involving minors, nudity without consent, or illegal content. You can block or report any person or tape in the app (Friends → ··· → Block / Report, or on a received tape → ··· → Report). We review reports within 24 hours and remove offending accounts' public profile and invite code.

You own your photos, sounds and text. You are responsible for what you send. Don't send content you don't have the right to share.

MemoryTape Pro is billed by Apple. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
